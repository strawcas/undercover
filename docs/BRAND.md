# Brand

## Fonts

From Google Fonts:

- **Headings and body**: Manrope
- **Accent (a tagline or a quote)**: Fraunces
- **Code, numbers and identifiers**: JetBrains Mono

## Colors

- **Primary**: Cyan `#22D3EE`, Deep Slate text
- **Secondary**: Tangerine `#FB923C`, Deep Slate text
- **Tertiary**: Lavender `#A78BFA`, Deep Slate text
- **Information**: Blue `#2563EB`, white text
- **Highlight or alert**: Raspberry `#E11D48`, white text
- **Ink on light backgrounds**: Deep Slate `#16243A`
- **Ink on dark backgrounds**: Frost `#DCE8F0`

Use Cyan, Tangerine, and Lavender when three things sit side by side, such as claim states or chart series. Add Blue as the fourth color and Raspberry as the fifth.

## Surfaces

- **Light mode**: page background `#F3F8FA`, white panels and cards `#FFFFFF`
- **Dark mode**: page background `#0C1424`, navy panels and cards `#17263A`

Panels are rounded with no outline. Use surface color differences to distinguish panels from the page background.

Cyan and Tangerine are too bright for small text on light backgrounds, so use darker shades for headings and body text. On dark backgrounds, use lighter shades when needed for readability.

Use Deep Slate text on Cyan, Tangerine, and Lavender backgrounds in both light and dark modes.

## Avoid

- Borders on one edge of a panel, and thin lines across the top of the page
- Heavy use of gradient fills

## Tone

Plain business English in US spelling. Short labels, no jargon and no exclamation marks. Money is in US dollars, such as $1,250.00.
